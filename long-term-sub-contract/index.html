East Prairie School District 73

Long-Term Substitute Teacher’s Contract

THIS CONTRACT is entered into by and between the Board of Education of East Prairie School District 73, Cook County, Illinois (“Board”) and (“Long-Term Substitute Teacher”), collectively referred to as the “Parties,” on this day of , 2026. In consideration of the mutual promises set forth below, the Parties agree as follows:

  1. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher is hereby employed beginning , 2026 as an at-will employee of the Board and, as such, he or she has no expectation of continued employment by the Board and his or her employment may be terminated by the Board at any time for any non-discriminatory reason, without restriction.
  2. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher will be paid at the daily Niles Township Substitute Cooperative rate of $170.00/day for the first forty-five (45) days of service* while filling the long-term substitute vacancy, less all applicable withholdings. Upon the forty-sixth (46th) day of continual service in the specified long-term vacancy requiring a substitute, the Long-Term Substitute Teacher will be paid at a rate of $302.20/day (reflecting a prorated first-year teacher salary of $55,000 for a 182-day school year). A lump sum payment will be issued to retroactively adjust the daily rate for the first forty-five (45) days of continuous service in this role to $302.20/day. If the Long-Term Substitute Teacher leaves the position or is terminated with cause prior to completing forty-five (45) days of service, they will not be entitled to the retroactive rate adjustment. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher will be entitled to no other compensation or benefits. In the event the Long-Term Substitute Teacher becomes eligible for health insurance coverage under applicable law, including but not limited to the Affordable Care Act, any such coverage provided shall be at one hundred percent (100%) of the Long-Term Substitute Teacher’s own cost.
  3. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher will perform the duties and responsibilities of a teacher and other duties which he or she may lawfully perform, as assigned by the Board.
  4. As a condition of employment, the Long-Term Substitute Teacher will authorize a fingerprint-based criminal history records check pursuant to Section 10-21.9 of the Illinois School Code (105 ILCS 5/10-21.9) and will also provide proof that he or she is licensed to serve as a long-term substitute teacher according to applicable Illinois law. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher acknowledges that he or she is a mandated reporter under the Illinois Abused and Neglected Child Reporting Act (325 ILCS 5/1 et seq.) and agrees to comply with all reporting requirements and complete required mandated reporter training as mandated by law.
  5. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher will abide by and be subject to all applicable Board policies while employed by the Board.
  6. This contract contains all of the terms agreed upon by the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements, arrangements and communications between the Parties concerning the Long-Term Substitute Teacher’s employment, whether oral or written.
  7. In the event the regular teacher returns prior to the anticipated end date of the long-term assignment, this contract and assignment shall immediately terminate without required advance notice. Should the certified staff member for whom the vacancy is being filled return from their leave before the 45-day retroactive adjustment, the $302.20/day rate will still be honored by the district for the days that were worked by the Long-Term Substitute.
  8. Contributions to the Teachers’ Retirement System of the State of Illinois (TRS) shall be remitted in accordance with statutory requirements and TRS rules governing substitute teacher eligibility and earnings reporting.
  9. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher is not eligible for paid sick leave, personal leave, or paid holidays and planned school closures. If an emergency school closure occurs on a day the Long-Term Substitute Teacher is scheduled to work, compensation will be provided for that day, provided the Long-Term Substitute Teacher performs required remote instruction or e-learning responsibilities as assigned.
  10. The Long-Term Substitute Teacher’s performance may be evaluated periodically by the building principal or designee.
